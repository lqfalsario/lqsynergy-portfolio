Sample analysis and next steps

  • Engagement: The illustrative Offer Reminder shows the strongest click rate (17.0%). Compare its subject line and call to action against earlier campaigns.
  • Deliverability: 61 sample bounces across 5,000 recipients suggest reviewing email-list quality before the next send.
  • Audience segmentation: Compare B2B and B2C recipients separately to identify meaningful performance differences.
  • Measurement: Track conversions and revenue separately when reliable attribution data becomes available; they are not inferred from clicks.
