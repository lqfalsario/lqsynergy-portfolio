What makes a record usable?

A reliable lead record should explain why a contact fits, where the information came from, how it was checked, and what the next action should be. A real campaign would add confirmed source URLs, contact fields, and a verification date.

What this sample does not show

This is a privacy-safe illustration. It contains no real prospect names, client data or invented campaign outcomes. The statuses in this demo describe an example process, not genuine verification work.
