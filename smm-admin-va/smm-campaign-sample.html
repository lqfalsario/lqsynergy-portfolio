04 / SHORT-FORM VIDEO

Fifteen seconds
of everyday calm.

A simple Reel storyboard that can be produced with a phone and natural light. This is a shot plan, not a produced video or a claim of professional footage.

00:00—00:03

Open with a hook

Wide shot of a tidy desk. Overlay text: “A pause looks good on you.”

00:03—00:07

Show the ritual

Close-up of a notebook opening, a cup being placed down, and warm morning light.

00:07—00:12

Highlight the feeling

Slow pans of simple everyday objects. Text: “Create a moment for yourself.”

00:12—00:15

End with a CTA

Neutral background with the concept brand name. Text: “Save this reminder.”

Reel sample caption

Your day can start with one calm moment. 🌿 No perfect routine needed—just one small thing that makes you feel more like yourself. Save this for the next time your morning gets busy. #SlowLiving #MorningRoutine #SundaySupply
