06 / PROCESS

Two workflows. One organized system.

A practical process for building qualified lead lists and running focused email campaigns from setup through follow-up.

LEAD GENERATION

From ICP to campaign-ready leads.

Research, verify, organize, and prepare the right contacts for focused outreach.

01

Define ICP

Clarify the audience, industry, location, role, qualification criteria, and campaign objective.

02

Source

Find relevant companies and decision-makers across trusted prospecting sources.

03

Verify

Validate available contact information, remove poor-fit records, and improve list quality.

04

Organize

Structure and segment the database so records are clear, usable, and ready for outreach.

05

Track

Document status, replies, next steps, and updates to support consistent follow-up.

EMAIL MARKETING

From campaign strategy to optimization.

Build focused campaigns with clear messaging, clean setup, testing, and performance review.

01

Plan

Define the campaign goal, audience, offer, send type, and the action each email should drive.

02

Segment

Group contacts by relevant criteria so messaging is more targeted and useful.

03

Create

Develop the subject line, email copy, CTA, and layout around one clear campaign objective.

04

Build & Launch

Set up the campaign, check links and formatting, complete QA, and schedule or send.

05

Analyze

Review engagement and response signals, document learnings, and improve the next campaign.
